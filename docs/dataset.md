# Dataset: GSE288996

The initial scope is limited to K562 Dasatinib and DMSO samples with RNA, ATAC,
and guideRNA information. iPSC samples, raw FASTQ processing, peak calling, and
fragment-level reprocessing are excluded.

Downloaded supplementary files belong in `data/raw/GSE288996/` and are ignored
by Git. The precise file-to-sample mapping and guide-assignment schema must be
documented here after the download completes and the files are inspected.
